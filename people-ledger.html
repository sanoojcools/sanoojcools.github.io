The Problem

Reference Checks Are Broken

I spent years at Amazon, AWS, and Swvl watching the same story repeat: a senior engineer wants to join a new company, but the reference check process fails them. Managers do not respond. HR refuses to share ratings. PDFs cannot be trusted. The result? Down-leveled offers, delayed hires, and talent leaving money on the table.

The $80K Problem

Sarah, a senior engineer at AWS, gets approached by Stripe. Her manager is on paternity leave and does not respond for 10 days. AWS HR will only confirm dates and title. Stripe cannot verify her "Exceeds Expectations" rating, so they down-level her from L7 to L5. She loses $80,000 in first-year compensation because a PDF could not be trusted and a phone call was not answered.

The Solution

Three Layers, One Protocol

🔒

Trust Layer

Employers cryptographically sign credentials. Employees own them forever. No take-backs.

🛡

Privacy Layer

Zero-knowledge proofs let you prove you are qualified without showing how qualified.

🤖

Intelligence Layer

An AI agent reads job descriptions, extracts requirements, and auto-generates proof requests.

How It Works

Sarahs Story, Replayed

1. Issue

AWS HR issues Sarah a credential when she joins. Her manager issues a performance credential at review time. Both are cryptographically signed and stored on-chain. Sarah owns them in her wallet.

2. AI Extraction

Her manager forwards the review email. An AI agent parses unstructured text, extracts name, title, level, rating, and skills, and structures it into a credential-ready format. No manual form-filling.

3. AI Matching

Stripe posts a Staff Engineer job. Their AI agent reads the description, extracts 6 requirements, and queries the chain for candidates who can generate valid ZK proofs for each.

4. ZK Proof

Sarah generates a proof: "My rating meets your threshold." Stripe sees the proof. They never see the number. Offer extended in 48 hours. No emails. No phone calls. No down-leveling.

My Unfair Advantage

Why I Built This

Most blockchain identity projects are built by engineers who have never issued a performance review. I have. At Amazon and AWS, I saw exactly what data employers want verified (title, level, rating, tenure) and what they do not (salary, project details, manager notes).

I also saw that the real bottleneck is not the blockchain -- it is the employer workflow. HR systems do not export clean JSON. They export messy emails and PDFs. That is why the AI Extraction layer matters: it turns unstructured HR documents into structured, verifiable credentials without changing employer behavior.
